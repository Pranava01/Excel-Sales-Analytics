# 📊 AtliQ Hardwares: Financial & Sales Analytics Portfolio

Welcome! This repository tracks the rapid global growth of **AtliQ Hardwares**, a consumer electronics company expanding across international markets[cite: 15, 18]. 

Between FY2019 and FY2021, AtliQ experienced massive scaling[cite: 17, 18]. To make sense of this growth, this project dives deep into Net Sales, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Gross Margins, and Sales Targets across global territories and retail channels[cite: 15, 17, 18].

---

## 💡 What’s Inside?

Instead of dumping raw numbers, I’ve broken down the data into focused analytical reports that answer specific business questions:

### 1. Global Financial Health (`country wise P & L finance report.pdf`)
* **What it covers:** Multi-year P&L breakdown by country (FY2019–FY2021)[cite: 15].
* **Key Metrics:** Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin ($), and Gross Margin percentage ($GM\%$)[cite: 15].
* **The Takeaway:** Shows how AtliQ’s top global markets—like the US ($87.8M) and India ($161.3M)—are scaling, while keeping an eye on profit margins[cite: 15].

### 2. Global Customer & Channel Performance (`Customer Market Report.pdf`)
* **What it covers:** Revenue patterns across global retail partners and direct channels[cite: 17].
* **Key Metrics:** 3-year sales performance and Year-over-Year ($21 \text{ vs } 20$) growth rates[cite: 17].
* **The Takeaway:** Highlights rapid expansion across direct-to-consumer platforms (AtliQ Exclusive, AtliQ e Store) as well as retail giants like Amazon, Leader, and Sage[cite: 17].

### 3. Top 10 Global Powerhouses (`top_10.pdf`)
* **What it covers:** A spotlight on AtliQ’s top 10 retail and e-commerce partners[cite: 20].
* **Key Metrics:** Combined FY2021 revenue of **$310.9M** (+265.3% YoY growth)[cite: 20].
* **The Takeaway:** Shows that a huge chunk of sales is driven by major players like Amazon ($82.1M), AtliQ Exclusive ($61.1M), and AtliQ e Store ($53.0M)[cite: 20].

### 4. Targets vs. Reality (`Market vs Target.pdf`)
* **What it covers:** A reality check comparing FY2021 actual sales against set performance targets[cite: 18].
* **Key Metrics:** Actual Sales, Target Gap ($), and Variance Percentage (%)[cite: 18].
* **The Takeaway:** Net Sales hit $598.9M globally[cite: 18]. While growth was huge, sales fell slightly short of ambitious targets by -$54.9M (-8.4% overall)[cite: 18].

### 5. Regional Spotlight: India (`Customer India report.pdf` & `salesreport_india.pdf`)
* **What it covers:** Deep-dive analysis focused specifically on the Indian market[cite: 16, 19].
* **Key Metrics:** Multi-year net sales broken down by domestic retail chains and e-commerce platforms[cite: 16, 19].
* **The Takeaway:** India surged from $30.8M in FY2019 to $161.3M in FY2021—an impressive **+324.0% total growth**, heavily led by Amazon, Flipkart, and AtliQ Exclusive[cite: 16, 19].

---

## 📈 Big-Picture Highlights

* **Explosive Revenue Growth:** Global Net Sales grew from **$87.5M in FY2019** to **$196.7M in FY2020**, reaching **$598.9M in FY2021**[cite: 17, 18].
* **Heavyweight Markets:** **India ($161.3M)** and the **USA ($87.8M)** remain AtliQ's largest regional revenue generators[cite: 15, 18].
* **Top-Heavy Customer Base:** The top 10 customers accounted for over half of total global sales in FY2021 ($310.9M out of $598.9M)[cite: 18, 20].
