# AtliQ Hardwares: Sales & Financial Performance Analytics

## Project Overview

**AtliQ Hardwares**, a rapidly growing global consumer electronics manufacturer, expanded its footprint across multiple international markets between Fiscal Years 2019 and 2021[cite: 1, 4]. To maintain high-level profitability and operational efficiency during rapid scaling, executive leadership requires clear, data-driven visibility into sales performance, cost structures, channel relationships, and target compliance.

This repository presents a comprehensive suite of financial and sales analytical reports designed to evaluate Net Sales, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Gross Margins, and target variance across global territories and major retail partners[cite: 1, 3, 4].

---

## Core Objectives

* **Financial Health Tracking:** Analyze Profit and Loss (P&L) statements across countries to monitor Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin growth, and Gross Margin percentages ($GM\%$) over FY2019–FY2021[cite: 1].
* **Channel & Customer Evaluation:** Assess revenue performance across global e-commerce and retail partners (e.g., Amazon, AtliQ e Store, Croma, Walmart) to identify top-performing sales drivers[cite: 2, 3].
* **Target Variance Analysis:** Measure actual Net Sales performance against predefined market targets for FY2021, pinpointing regional shortfalls and expansion opportunities[cite: 4].
* **Regional Deep Dives:** Conduct detailed market-specific analyses, particularly within high-impact regions like India, North America, and Europe, to support localized commercial strategies[cite: 1, 2].
* **Strategic Decision Support:** Empower key decision-makers with actionable financial intelligence to improve product pricing, promotional strategies, and supply chain allocation[cite: 1, 4].

---

## Included Reports & Deliverables

### 1. Country-Wise P&L Finance Report
* **Purpose:** Evaluates financial performance by country across FY2019, FY2020, and FY2021[cite: 1].
* **Key Metrics:** Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin, and Year-over-Year (21 vs 20) percentage changes[cite: 1].
* **Core Takeaway:** Tracks how top markets—such as the USA, India, South Korea, and Canada—are scaling revenue while maintaining healthy gross margins[cite: 1].

### 2. Global Customer Market Report
* **Purpose:** Tracks Net Sales across global customer accounts and retail channels[cite: 3].
* **Key Metrics:** 3-year sales trends and percentage growth across primary global partners[cite: 3].
* **Core Takeaway:** Highlights rapid expansion across both direct-to-consumer channels (AtliQ e Store, AtliQ Exclusive) and key third-party retailers (Amazon, Leader, Sage)[cite: 3].

### 3. India Market Customer Report
* **Purpose:** Provides a dedicated performance deep dive into the Indian domestic market[cite: 2].
* **Key Metrics:** Revenue breakdown across local retail chains and e-commerce platforms[cite: 2].
* **Core Takeaway:** Amazon, AtliQ Exclusive, and AtliQ e Store emerged as significant growth engines in the domestic market, driving revenue from $30.8M in FY2019 to $161.3M in FY2021[cite: 2].

### 4. Market Performance vs. Target Report
* **Purpose:** Compares FY2021 actual sales against corporate revenue targets across all operational markets[cite: 4].
* **Key Metrics:** 2021 Target Variance ($) and Variance Percentage (%)[cite: 4].
* **Core Takeaway:** Across all markets, total Net Sales reached $598.9M against targets, revealing a global variance gap of -$54.9M (-8.4%)[cite: 4].

---

## Key Business Insights

* **Macro Revenue Growth:** Overall Net Sales grew from **$87.5M in FY2019** to **$196.7M in FY2020**, reaching **$598.9M in FY2021** (a 304.5% increase over FY2020)[cite: 3, 4].
* **Top Revenue Drivers:** The **USA ($87.8M)** and **India ($161.3M)** stand out as the primary market growth drivers in FY2021[cite: 1, 4].
* **Target Shortfall Analysis:** While overall growth remained strong, every market experienced a minor target shortfall in FY2021, ranging from -4.0% in Japan to -15.3% in Poland, indicating a need for more precise target forecasting[cite: 4].
* **Gross Margin Dynamics:** Countries like the UK (+32.2%), Japan (+25.9%), and South Korea (+20.5%) saw significant GM% expansions in 21 vs 20, reflecting improved pricing power and product mix[cite: 1].
